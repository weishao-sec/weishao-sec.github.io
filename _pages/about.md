I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of California, Davis, advised by Prof. [Houman Homayoun](https://houman.ece.ucdavis.edu/) and Prof. [Setareh Rafatirad](https://web.cs.ucdavis.edu/~srafatir/).

My research focuses on systems security, with an emphasis on understanding security-relevant behavior hidden behind system abstractions. My recent work spans cloud and serverless security, LLM agent security, and wearable authentication, including problems in isolation, co-location, resource management, and long-horizon security evaluation.
